How to use Review Desk

Upload screenshots, send one link, and get feedback pinned to the exact spot on the design. This guide walks through everything, from a quick demo to running a space with your team.

Just trying it out?Start a demo. No account needed. Someone sent you a link?See how to leave feedback. Running a real project?Get a space and invite your team.

1How it works

Everything lives in a space. A space holds sections (for example “Sign-up flow” or “Checkout”), and each section holds screenshots. People comment on a whole section, or click a screenshot to pin a comment to one spot.

2Try a demo

A demo is a private, temporary space for trying Review Desk alone. You don’t need an account.

  1. On the home page, click Start a demo.
  2. Your demo space opens. You own it, and it’s deleted automatically after 24 hours. Click Get started.
  3. The blue bar at the top shows the time left and the storage used. Get a permanent space takes you to the request form.

The demo belongs to the browser that started it. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, you lose access to it. To come back later in the same browser, use Continue your demo on the home page.

3Sections and screenshots

  1. Click New section (or Create first section). Give it a title, and add a description to tell reviewers what to focus on.
  2. Add screenshots in any of three ways:
    • click Upload and choose files,
    • drag files onto the dashed box, or
    • copy a screenshot and paste it with Ctrl/⌘ + V.

PNG, JPG, GIF and WebP files up to 15 MB each are accepted. A space can have up to 40 sections. Use Edit to rename a section or change its description, and the pencil next to the space name to rename the space.

4Pin feedback to a spot

  1. Click a screenshot to open it. Comments are listed on the right.
  2. Click anywhere on the screenshot. A pin appears where you clicked. Type your comment and click Post.

Each pinned comment gets a number that matches its pin. Hover over a pin to preview the comment, and click it to jump to the comment. The toolbar can switch between fit to screen and actual size, and can copy a link straight to this screenshot.

5Share with reviewers

Reviewers don’t need an account. They only need the review link.

  1. Click Share at the top of the space. The Reviewers tab shows the review link and the review code. Click Copy link and send it any way you like.
  2. If you’re signed in, you can also email the link. Type an address and click Send.

Copy link on a section, or the link icon in the viewer, copies a link that opens that exact section or screenshot.

Cut off access

To stop old links from working, open the ⋯ menu and choose New review code…. Everyone with the old link loses access until you send them the new one.

6Reviewing a design

This part is for people who were sent a review link.

  1. Open the link. You’ll see the sections on the left and the screenshots in the middle. Sections with a number in orange have open comments.
  2. To comment on the whole flow, use Section feedback under the screenshots. The first time, type your name. It’s remembered for next time.
  3. To comment on one detail, open a screenshot and click the spot. Your pins are numbered in the order they were added.

Review Desk works on phones too: tap a screenshot to open it, then tap a spot to pin a comment.

If you’re signed in, your comments show your account name with a ✓, so nobody can post under your name. Without an account, the name is whatever you type. You can change it in the ⋯ menu.

7Reply and resolve

Owners and editors keep track of feedback as it comes in.

  1. Click Refresh to load the newest comments. Screenshots with open comments show an orange badge.
  2. Click Reply under a comment to answer it in a thread.
  3. Click Resolve when a comment has been dealt with. It’s hidden from the list and its pin disappears.
  4. Click Show resolved comments to see them again (their pins turn green), and Reopen to make one open again.

8Get your own space

A space is permanent, has more storage, and lets you work with a team.

  1. Click Sign in at the top right. You can use Google, GitHub, Microsoft or an email link. Your account menu (top right) has your profile and a list of your spaces.
  2. Click Request a space. Tell us what you’ll review and click Send request.
  3. Your request appears on the home page as Waiting for approval. You’ll get an email when an admin approves it.
  4. Once it’s approved, the space appears under Your spaces, and you’re its owner. Open it from any device by signing in.
  5. Create sections and upload screenshots as in step 3. The bar at the top shows how much storage you’ve used.

9Add team members

Owners can invite editors (who upload screenshots and manage feedback) and other owners (who can also manage the team).

  1. Click Share and open the Team tab. Type your teammate’s email address, choose Editor or Owner, and click Invite.
  2. They get an email with an invite link. Until they accept, the invite is listed as pending. Click Cancel to withdraw it.
  3. After they accept, they’re listed as a member. Use Make owner / Make editor to change their role, or Remove to take away access.

Invites are single-use and only work for the email address they were sent to. If someone forwards an invite, it won’t work for anyone else. A space always keeps at least one owner.

10Join a space as an editor

This part is for people who were invited to a team.

  1. Open the link in your invite email. Click Sign in to accept, and sign in with the email address the invite was sent to.
  2. Click Accept invite. If you’re signed in with a different email address, click Use another account.
  3. The space opens, and it’s now listed under Your spaces on the home page.

To leave a space, open the ⋯ menu and choose Leave this space….

11Site admin

Site admins approve requests and look after every space. The admin console needs two-step verification turned on for your account. Open it from your account menu → Site admin.

  1. Requests lists new space requests. Click Approve or Reject (you can give a reason, which the requester sees).
  2. When approving, choose the space name and its storage.
  3. The space is created and the owner is emailed. You also get the links, in case you need to pass them on.
  4. Spaces shows each space’s owner and storage use. From here you can open a space, view its keys, change its storage, issue a new key, or delete it.
  5. Demos lists the demos currently running. Activity is a log of security-relevant actions, such as approvals, invites and role changes, kept for a year.

Use New space at the top to create a space for someone directly, without a request.

12Tips and questions

Keyboard shortcuts in the viewer

← →Previous / next screenshot
EscRemove the pin you’re placing, or close the viewer
Ctrl/⌘ + VPaste a screenshot into the open section (owners and editors)
I don’t see new comments.

Click Refresh at the top of the space. New comments also load by themselves when you come back to the tab after a minute or more away.

My demo has gone.

Demos are deleted 24 hours after they start, together with their screenshots and comments. You can start a new one, or request a space for anything you want to keep.

The review link says the code no longer works.

The owner issued a new review code, which stops old links from working. Ask them for the new link.

My invite doesn’t work.

Invites expire after 7 days, work only once, and only for the email address they were sent to. Make sure you’re signed in with that address. If it has expired, ask the owner to send a new one.

I have a space key that starts with “sk-”.

Spaces made before accounts use a space key. Paste it under Have a link? on the home page, then sign in and choose Make your account the owner in the bar at the top. After that, the key stops working and you manage the space with your account.

What does “Forget on this device” do?

It removes the space from this browser’s list. It doesn’t delete anything. For a space on your account, it will still be under Your spaces.

The space is full.

Delete screenshots you no longer need (open one, then click the bin icon), or ask the site admin for more storage.
